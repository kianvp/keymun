Our Conference

What is KeyMUN?

KeyMUN is the Model United Nations conference of Keystone School, Hyderabad — three days in which the school becomes a working simulation of the United Nations, run entirely by its students.

Delegates take on the seat of a country or a figure they have never been, argue a crisis they did not create, and try to get a resolution past a room that has every reason to say no. Six committees run at once, from disarmament to a joint crisis cabinet.

"We prepare students not merely to win the room — but to understand it."

It is our proudest tradition: a weekend where learning to listen matters as much as learning to speak, and where two hundred delegates leave arguing better than they arrived.
